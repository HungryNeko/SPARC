Measured once on held-out LibriSpeech test trials with this pipeline. The language model was run at full precision for the table; the 8-bit version used in your browser changed SPARC log-likelihood ratios by a median of 0.013 and changed no decisions on 200 checked trials. Accuracy weights same- and different-speaker trials equally. Cllr and calibration error need probabilities, so they do not apply to the raw score. Lower is better for EER, Cllr and calibration error.

  • The examples are LibriSpeech test pairs drawn at random, two from the same speaker and two from different speakers; they were not chosen for being easy.
  • Calibration was fitted on LibriSpeech development data (English read speech). Recordings from other conditions may be scored less reliably — calibration is specific to its target domain.
  • Speaker model: CAMPPlus (3D-Speaker, Apache-2.0). Voice activity: Silero VAD (MIT). Language: SpeechBrain VoxLingua107 ECAPA (Apache-2.0, 8-bit). Example audio: LibriSpeech (CC BY 4.0, Panayotov et al., 2015).
